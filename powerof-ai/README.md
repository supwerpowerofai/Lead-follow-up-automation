# powerof-ai — Practical AI Blueprint Studio

> Turn a rough business problem into a structured AI implementation brief.

**SUPERPOWER AI** · Flagship concept · Early prototype

## What it does

PowerOfAI is a browser-only prompt and workflow brief builder. Enter a business problem, target user, desired outcome, constraints, and tools; it produces a structured brief you can copy into your preferred AI assistant or use to plan a prototype.

It does **not** call an AI model, send data to a server, or promise that generated plans are correct. It structures your inputs into a more useful starting brief.

## Quick start

1. Open `index.html` in a modern browser.
2. Fill in the workflow details.
3. Select **Build my blueprint**.
4. Review the result, then copy it for your chosen AI assistant or implementation process.

No dependencies, API keys, build step, or account required.

## Privacy

The demo runs in your browser. It does not transmit form data or use external APIs. Do not enter secrets or confidential customer information into prompts you later share with third-party services.

## Current status

Prototype. The brief builder is implemented as a static page. Model integration, saved projects, team collaboration, and automated execution are not included.
