# Follow-Up Message Templates

These are editable starting points, not messages sent automatically. Confirm the service, availability, price, and preferred communication channel before use.

## 1. First response
Hi [Name], thanks for contacting [Business] about [Service]. We’ve received your enquiry. Could you share [one relevant detail] so we can guide you correctly?

## 2. After sending details
Hi [Name], just checking whether the information about [Service] was helpful. If you’d like to proceed, I can help with the next step.

## 3. Appointment confirmation
Hi [Name], your [Service/appointment] is scheduled for [Date] at [Time], subject to the business confirming this booking. Please reply if anything needs to change.

## 4. Appointment reminder
Hi [Name], a reminder about your [Service/appointment] on [Date] at [Time]. Please let us know if you need to reschedule.

## 5. Final check-in
Hi [Name], I’m checking in once more about your enquiry for [Service]. If now isn’t the right time, no problem. We’ll close the follow-up for now; you’re welcome to contact us if you need help later.

## 6. Stop-contact acknowledgement
Thanks for letting us know. We’ll stop follow-up messages about this enquiry.

## Usage rules
- Replace every bracketed placeholder and review the message before sending.
- Never claim a booking is confirmed until the business confirms it.
- Do not use fake urgency, misleading scarcity, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Honour opt-outs promptly.
- Record that a message was sent only after it actually was.
