## Next qualification criteria
Prioritise a prospect only if they confirm at least one real issue:
- enquiries are spread across multiple channels;
- pending follow-ups are hard to track;
- staff cannot easily see who should call whom today;
- the owner is willing to test a small process improvement.

Do not promise more sales. Offer a small paid pilot only after the problem, scope, and success measures are agreed.
