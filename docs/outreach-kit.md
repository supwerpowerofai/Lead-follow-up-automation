## Phone opener

“Hi, I’m Aryan from OPERONIX AI. I’ll keep it brief — I’m trying to understand how your team tracks enquiries that ask for a quote but don't book straight away. Is that something you manage, or is there an operations person I should speak with?”

If they are open to talking:
- “Where do enquiries arrive from today?”
- “How do you know which pending enquiries need a follow-up today?”
- “What, if anything, slips through the cracks?”
- “Would a simple shared tracker and daily pending list be useful, or do you already have this handled?”

## If they ask for price

“I'm considering a 7-day pilot at ₹5,000, but I’d first confirm that there’s a real problem to solve and agree exactly what is included. It would start with a tracker, follow-up templates, and a daily review process; live integrations or automatic customer messages are not included unless separately agreed and tested.”

## Follow-up etiquette
- Contact only through an appropriate public business channel.
- Personalise the opening line and keep the first message brief.
- If there is no response, at most one polite follow-up after several business days, unless the recipient says otherwise.
- Stop immediately if asked not to contact them.
- Do not claim you reviewed their internal workflow, identified lost leads, or built a custom system for them.
- Do not represent a prospect as a customer or imply endorsement.
