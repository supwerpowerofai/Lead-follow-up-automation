# OPERONIX AI — Lead Follow-Up Pilot

**Proposed offer:** 7-day fixed-scope pilot  
**Proposed price:** ₹5,000, agreed before work starts  
**Status:** Offer draft; not yet sold or delivered.

## Intended customer
A local service business that receives enquiries but struggles to track pending responses and next follow-up actions.

## Included
- Short discovery call and review of the current enquiry process.
- One simple lead tracker using a free spreadsheet tool selected with the client.
- Agreed lead statuses and next-action fields.
- Editable follow-up message templates.
- Daily pending/overdue review process.
- Walkthrough and a brief end-of-pilot review against agreed measures.

## Not included unless separately agreed
- Paid software subscriptions or messaging charges.
- Custom CRM development.
- Bulk messaging or unsolicited outreach.
- Guaranteed bookings, sales, or revenue.
- Live API integrations or automatic customer messages before access, permissions, and tests are confirmed.
- Ongoing support beyond the agreed pilot.

## Before accepting payment
1. Confirm the business's actual problem and current lead volume.
2. Agree deliverables, start/end dates, payment terms, and acceptance criteria in writing.
3. Confirm data access, privacy responsibilities, and who approves messages.
4. Explain that results depend on the business's lead quality, offer, response speed, and other factors.
5. Do not claim the system is already deployed; demonstrate the documented prototype and explain what remains to be configured.

## Possible pilot measures
Choose measures with the client before starting:
- Share of open leads with a next action.
- Number of overdue leads.
- Follow-up completion rate.
- Time to first response, if reliable timestamps exist.

## Honest positioning
This is an operational improvement pilot, not a promise of additional sales. Only report results supported by records gathered during the pilot.
