# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people of different experience levels and backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Give actionable feedback focused on the work, not personal attacks.
- Welcome beginner questions and explain technical terms when practical.
- Respect privacy; never post credentials, private customer data, or personal information.
- Disagree constructively and accept that maintainers may decline a change with an explanation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or block participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub profile. This project is small and response times are not guaranteed.
